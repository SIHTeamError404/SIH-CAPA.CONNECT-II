CAPACITY CONNECT



CAPACITY CONNECT is proposed as a solution to this exact problem — an AI-enabled, competency-based Digital Capacity Building and Learning Management Portal that brings trainees, trainers, and administrators together on a single, integrated system. At its core, the platform uses intelligent algorithms to identify each trainee's individual skill gaps, recommend personalized courses and assessments tailored to their role, and continuously track their learning journey through diagnostic tests and performance analytics. What sets CAPACITY CONNECT apart from conventional Learning Management Systems is its emphasis on measurable competency development rather than mere course completion — it maps role-specific competencies, generates organization-wide skill-gap analytics, and gives administrators actionable insights to make better training and workforce decisions. Built on a scalable and modular technical foundation — a FastAPI-powered backend, a MySQL database managed through SQLAlchemy ORM, and a responsive HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, and Tailwind CSS frontend — the platform is designed not only to solve today's training fragmentation problem but also to scale toward future capabilities such as AI-generated assessments, an intelligent learning assistant, and adaptive, personalized learning pathways. In essence, CAPACITY CONNECT transforms training from a generic, one-size-fits-all exercise into a data-driven, personalized, and outcome-focused capacity-building system.
