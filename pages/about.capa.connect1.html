ABOUT CAPACITY CONNECT

ABOUT

CAPACITY CONNECT** is an AI-enabled, competency-based Digital Capacity Building and Learning Management Portal designed to bring **trainees, trainers, and administrators together on a single integrated platform.

Unlike conventional Learning Management Systems that primarily focus on course completion, CAPACITY CONNECT focuses on **measurable competency development and continuous skill improvement**. The platform uses intelligent algorithms to identify individual skill gaps, map role-specific competencies, and recommend personalized courses, learning paths, and assessments based on each trainee’s needs.

CAPACITY CONNECT also provides **organization-wide skill-gap analytics and performance insights**, enabling administrators and trainers to monitor learning progress, identify competency gaps, and make informed training and workforce-development decisions.

Through diagnostic assessments, personalized learning, continuous performance tracking, and data-driven analytics, CAPACITY CONNECT creates a connected learning ecosystem that helps organizations **build the right skills, measure real competency growth, and strengthen workforce capabilities**.
